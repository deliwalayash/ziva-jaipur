How to Calculate Volumetric Weight?

International air express carriers calculate freight charges based on the higher of the parcel's Actual Gross Weight or its Volumetric (Dimensional) Weight. Use our instant calculator below to measure your package accurately.

Volumetric Weight Calculator

Volumetric Weight: 0.03 kg
Volume: 125 cm³ | Formula: (5 × 5 × 5) ÷ 5000

*Courier charge is calculated on whichever is higher: Actual Gross Weight vs Volumetric Weight (0.03 kg).
