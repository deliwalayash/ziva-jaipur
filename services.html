Prohibited & Restricted Items Guide

In strict compliance with IATA aviation safety guidelines and international customs laws, the following items cannot be shipped via air courier.

Flammable liquids, perfumes, aerosols, and spray cans
Loose lithium metal batteries & standalone power banks
Currency notes, coins, bearer checks & counterfeit currency
Explosives, weapons, ammunition & replica toy guns
Narcotics, banned psychotropic substances & illegal drugs
Toxic chemicals, corrosives, radioactive items & poisons
