Important Pricing & Billing Information

1. Weight Calculation: International air freight is charged on the higher of Actual Physical Weight or Volumetric Dimensional Weight (Length × Width × Height in cm / 5000 as per IATA standards).

2. Rate Tiers: Base rates displayed on cards (*per Kg) apply to bulk shipments (50 Kg and above). For smaller shipments (0.5kg - 49kg), documents, or special food/medicine items, tiered retail pricing applies.

3. Doorstep Pickup & Packing: Free doorstep pickup across Jaipur and standard 7-layer export box packing are included in all consignments at no additional charge.

4. Customs Duties & Remote Areas: Rates exclude destination customs duties or local import taxes if assessed by overseas customs authorities, which are payable by the recipient upon clearance.

How Courier Weight Is Measured

Understanding Volumetric Weight

International airlines calculate cargo space based on both physical weight and box dimensions. If a parcel is large but lightweight (e.g. puffed snacks, soft toys, bulky jackets), dimensional weight is used.

Standard IATA Volumetric Formula:
Volumetric Weight (Kg) = (Length × Width × Height in cm) / 5000
Example: A 50cm × 40cm × 30cm box = 60,000 / 5000 = 12 Kg volumetric weight.
