Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear, Fair Guidelines on Consignment Cancellation and Fee Adjustments

Home / Refunds

1. Pickup Cancellation

Doorstep pickup bookings in Jaipur can be cancelled or rescheduled free of cost at any time prior to the collection boy's arrival at your premises. Simply call 83060 06570 or message on WhatsApp 7300030628.

2. Cancellation Prior to Flight Dispatch

If a parcel has been picked up, packed, and weighed at our Govindpura branch but has not yet been processed for international air express manifesting and airline handover, the sender may request a shipment return. In such cases, freight charges will be refunded in full minus nominal domestic collection and box packing expenses (₹200 - ₹500 depending on materials used).

3. Non-Cancellable Stages

Once an international Air Waybill (AWB) is validated, customs clearance completed, and the consignment transferred into international air transit custody, shipments cannot be cancelled or recalled.

4. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are credited back via the original payment method (UPI, Bank Transfer, or Cash) within 2 to 4 working business days.

5. Contact Desk

For billing adjustments, write to new_govindpura.raj@fr.dtdc.com or call +91 9929674710.
